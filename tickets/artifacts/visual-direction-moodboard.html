Decision: A, Blue, evolved (Bibi, 2026-10-17). The identity is kept for continuity and recognition. The readability risk is handled with a Deep reading column on case-study pages (see visual-direction.md).

Original recommendation: B, Lab notebook. It serves both primary audiences without losing the secondary one. Case studies, code prototypes and craft builds all fit one "experiments and notes" frame, and it gives "share knowledge" a home. The v3 blue and the star carry over as ink and a small mark, so the identity changes without starting from nothing. From C, borrow the spec tables for craft projects.
