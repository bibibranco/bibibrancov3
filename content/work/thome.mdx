## The gap Covid-19 made wider

Covid-19 changed the way kids access education, and widened the gap between private and public schools in Brazil.

## Familiar on purpose

I designed Thomé as the final project for my Graphic Design degree at Universidade Federal de Pelotas. Its main goal was an experience that already felt familiar to most users: a calendar, messages with teachers, and conversations by subject.
