I recently rediscovered my passion for working with tangible things. It's my way of touching grass after a whole day in front of screens.

My grandfather taught me how to grow and care for plants, how to build benches, and how to cut glass, which was his trade. Only after he passed, and after some time in ITP's shop, did I make peace with that side of me. Back home, I set up my own shop, and now I proudly have several pieces I built myself around my place.
