## Listening first

As the only designer leading a complete redesign of the app, I started by listening to people across the company, from the CEO to individual contributors in marketing. I wanted to understand their pains, their audience and their goals.

## From low fidelity to a new interface

I showed low-fidelity proposals to solve their problems, researched, and came up with a new interface: straightforward, user-friendly and technically feasible.
