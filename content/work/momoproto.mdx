## Why a prototype hub

Prototypes at momoGood are clickable React apps, not static screens. momoProto puts all of them under one app, so anyone on the team can find, open and compare them in one place.

## One contract for every prototype

Every prototype has to follow the same design-system contract: the shared tokens, primitives and type ramp. An automated `ds:check` gate checks it, so a prototype that drifts from the system gets caught before anyone reviews it.
