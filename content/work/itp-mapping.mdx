By combining my design background with projection mapping, I made a visual representation of my human interactions during ITP Camp, quantified and qualified.

<Figure
  src="./map.jpg"
  alt="Black-and-white photo of someone in a cap working on a laptop at ITP Camp"
  caption="TODO: caption"
/>

For 20 days I logged how many interactions I had each day in a spreadsheet. I also tracked my overall mood and other outside factors, like whether I'd had a pre-drink before going to camp, to see whether the number of people I talked to in a day was connected to how I felt that day.

Those insights became 3D prisms, one per day of camp: a physical chart where the Y axis was the days of camp and the X axis the number of interactions per day.
